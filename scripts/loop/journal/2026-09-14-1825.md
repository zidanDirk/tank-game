## What it does

- Tracks `killStreak` and `lastKillAt` on `GameManager`.
- `STREAK.window = 4s` and `STREAK.maxMult = 4` (config.js).
- On enemy kill, `streakMult = min(killStreak, maxMult)`; awarded score is `tank.score * streakMult`.
- Streak resets to 0 when the player loses a life OR when `time - lastKillAt > STREAK.window` (decayed in `tick`).
- HUD `#streak-badge` (gold) appears next to the score when `streakMult > 1`; pulses faster and turns orange at ×3+.
- `snapshot()` exposes `killStreak` and `streakMult` additively (no rename, no removal).
